# Exploring public-private data sharing practices through the lens of Open Data Ecosystems: A multiple case study on municipal and regional freight logistics

**Authors**: John Larsson, Johan Linåker, Per Runeson
**Date**: 2026-11-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2611.12440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12440)

## Summary

This paper explores public-private data sharing practices within municipal and regional last-mile freight logistics through a multiple-case study in Sweden framed by Open Data Ecosystems. The authors use semi-structured interviews, thematic analysis, and a follow-up validation workshop across three cases to examine socio-technical aspects such as platform design, actor roles, sensitive data handling, and procurement. The findings are synthesized into an actor model, a conceptual model, and seven proposition sketches that highlight the role of public actors as neutral keystone orchestrators.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted a multiple-case study across three Swedish municipal and regional freight logistics cases exploring public-private data sharing practices.
- Identified key socio-technical aspects shaping data sharing in public-private freight logistics, including platform design, actor roles, sensitive data handling, data quality, and procurement processes.
- Synthesized an actor model and a conceptual model of Open Data Ecosystems for Public-Private Freight (ODE-PPF) along with seven proposition sketches for future research.
- Demonstrated that public actors can act as neutral keystone actors orchestrating ecosystems and facilitating data sharing through procurement governance.

## Limitations

Limited to qualitative multiple-case study methodology within Swedish municipal and regional contexts.
