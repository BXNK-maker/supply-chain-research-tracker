# Exploring public-private data sharing practices through the lens of Open Data Ecosystems: A multiple case study on municipal and regional freight logistics

**Authors**: John Larsson, Johan Linåker, Per Runeson
**Date**: 2026-11-09
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2611.12440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12440)

## Summary

This paper explores public-private data sharing practices within Open Data Ecosystems (ODE) for municipal and regional last-mile freight logistics through a multiple-case study in Sweden. The authors identify key socio-technical aspects—such as platform design, actor roles, sensitive data handling, data quality, and procurement—that shape data sharing. Findings are synthesized into an actor model, a conceptual model, and seven proposition sketches, highlighting the role of public actors as neutral keystone orchestrators.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted a multiple-case study involving three municipal and regional freight logistics cases in Sweden to examine public-private data sharing practices.
- Synthesized findings into an actor model and a conceptual model highlighting socio-technical aspects such as platform design, actor roles, sensitive data handling, data quality, and procurement.
- Derived seven proposition sketches to establish a foundational framework for future research on Open Data Ecosystems in public-private freight (ODE-PPF).

## Limitations

Limited to municipal and regional last-mile freight logistics cases within Sweden; requires further empirical validation across broader geographic and institutional contexts.
