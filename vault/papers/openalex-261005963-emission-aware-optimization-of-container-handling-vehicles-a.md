# Emission-Aware Optimization of Container Handling Vehicles: A HaminaKotka Port Case Study

**Authors**: Hafiz Majid Hussain, Juha Haakana, Mehar Ullah
**Date**: 2026-10-05
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.05963](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05963)

## Summary

This paper proposes a data-driven fleet scheduling framework to optimize landside container handling vehicle operations for decarbonization at the Port of HaminaKotka. By formulating the task as a linear programming workload allocation problem that reallocates operating hours away from carbon-intensive vehicles, the approach achieves a 31% reduction in total CO2eq emissions and a 9.4% reduction in total energy consumption without reducing aggregate workload. Additionally, the study reviews IoT-enabler technologies for digital transformation and real-time decision-making in smart ports.

## Key Contributions

- Introduces a linear programming workload allocation framework for container handling vehicles that minimizes total CO2eq emissions and energy consumption while maintaining total aggregate workload.
- Demonstrates via a case study on a two-year dataset from 77 vehicles at the Port of HaminaKotka that shifting utilization away from high-intensity vehicles achieves an approximately 31% reduction in total CO2eq and a 9.4% reduction in total energy consumption.
- Provides a review of IoT-enabler technologies supporting real-time decision-making and decarbonization in smart port environments.
