# Routes planning in a condominium company using mathematical optimization

**Authors**: Lara Rocha, Pedro Martins
**Date**: 2026-10-14
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.20080](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20080)

## Summary

This paper addresses the daily route planning problem for employees in a condominium management company, aiming to minimize total travel distance and lunch break penalties. The model incorporates employee skill matching, customer time windows, maximum working times, and conditional lunch breaks spanning morning and afternoon periods. Distance and time matrices are obtained via Microsoft Azure APIs, and optimized routes are visualized using Bing Maps. Results demonstrate significant operational improvements in real-world condominium service management.

## Key Contributions

- Formulates and solves a real-world route planning problem for employees of a condominium management company using mathematical optimization with skill-matching constraints.
- Incorporates practical operational constraints including customer time windows, maximum working hours, and conditional lunch break scheduling with associated penalties.
- Demonstrates operational advantages and distance minimization on real-world data utilizing Microsoft Azure APIs for distance/time collection and Bing Maps for visualization.
