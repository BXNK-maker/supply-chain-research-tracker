# Scalable Reference Electrical Architectures for Modern Data Centers

**Authors**: Kiran Kumar Challa, Yan, Zhou, Kumarsinh Jhala
**Date**: 2026-11-11
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2611.15712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15712)

## Summary

This paper presents scalable, vendor-neutral reference electrical architectures for colocation, hyperscale, and AI data centers to address the growing power demands of modern computing infrastructures. The proposed framework integrates modular Power PODs, hierarchical high/medium/low voltage distribution, and energy storage systems into a unified planning methodology. By developing three representative Power POD configurations, the framework facilitates utility interconnection studies, equipment demand forecasting, and transmission planning for multi-gigawatt campuses.

## Key Contributions

- Presents scalable, vendor-neutral reference electrical architectures for colocation, hyperscale, and AI data centers ranging from tens of megawatts to multi-gigawatt campuses.
- Integrates modular Power PODs, hierarchical HV/MV/LV distribution, standby generation, UPS, and BESS into a unified planning methodology.
- Develops three representative Power POD configurations to support systematic capacity expansion while maintaining consistent electrical topology and equipment sizing.
