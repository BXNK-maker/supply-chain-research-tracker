## Summary

This paper presents an integrated seven-layer omics characterization of 60 US retail milk samples, spanning genomics, transcriptomics, peptidomics, proteomics, lipidomics, metabolomics, and glycomics. The authors identified and quantified thousands of compounds, introduced the web-accessible Dairy Molecule Database (DMD) to link compound variations with genetic and product-level factors, and experimentally validated multiple computationally predicted antimicrobial peptides against bacterial pathogens.

## Key Contributions

- Conducted an integrated seven-layer omics characterization (genomics, transcriptomics, peptidomics, proteomics, lipidomics, metabolomics, and glycomics) across 60 US retail milk samples.
- Identified 6,714 compounds and quantified 5,288 with absolute concentrations, introducing absolute concentration estimates for 5,220 previously unprofiled compounds.
- Developed the Dairy Molecule Database (DMD), a web-accessible resource linking milk compound concentrations to SNPs, miRNAs, and supply-chain factors.
- Validated 35 out of 54 computationally predicted antimicrobial peptides, finding 6 with IC50 values below 256 ug/mL against A. baumannii.
