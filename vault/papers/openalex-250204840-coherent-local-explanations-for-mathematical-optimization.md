# Coherent local explanations for mathematical optimization

**Authors**: Daan Otto, Jannis Kurtz, Ş. İlker Birbil, Coherent local explanations for mathematical optimization
**Date**: 2026-10-19
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2502.04840](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04840)

## Summary

Explaining decisions in mathematical optimization is challenging because standard XAI methods like LIME ignore the underlying problem structure and feasibility constraints. To address this, the authors introduce Coherent Local Explanations for Mathematical Optimization (CLEMO), a method designed to simultaneously explain objective values and decision variables while ensuring coherence with the problem structure. Experiments across shortest path, knapsack, and vehicle routing problems illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach.

## Key Contributions

- Introduced CLEMO (Coherent Local Explanations for Mathematical Optimization) to explain both objective values and decision variables in mathematical optimization problems while maintaining structural coherence.
- Demonstrated that existing XAI methods like LIME fail to account for underlying optimization structure, leading to unreliable outcomes.
- Evaluated CLEMO on shortest path, knapsack, and vehicle routing problems, demonstrating its effectiveness in generating coherent local explanations.
