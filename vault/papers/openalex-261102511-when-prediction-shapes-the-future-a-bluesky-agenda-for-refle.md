# When Prediction Shapes the Future: A BlueSky Agenda for Reflexive Time Series Analysis

**Authors**: Tao Xie, Yiqun Zhang, Yiu‐ming Cheung
**Date**: 2026-11-02
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2611.02511](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02511)

## Summary

This paper introduces a BlueSky research agenda for reflexive time-series analysis, addressing the challenges of AI-shaped trajectories where predictions and subsequent interventions alter future observations in operational systems. The authors argue that reusing such feedback-driven records as passive data leads to systematic model drift, as models misread intervention effects as natural phenomena. To resolve this sequence-prediction coupling, the agenda establishes closed-loop provenance, sequence identification under causal assumptions, and closed-loop evaluation to separate intrinsic dynamics from policy-driven effects.

## Key Contributions

- Advocates a research agenda for reflexive time-series analysis addressing AI-shaped trajectories where predictions alter future observations in closed-loop systems.
- Formulates closed-loop provenance to track operational context including predictions, policies, and actions across temporal horizons.
- Proposes sequence identification and closed-loop evaluation to decouple intrinsic process prediction from policy-driven intervention effects under explicit causal assumptions.

## Limitations

The paper presents a conceptual BlueSky agenda rather than a fully implemented algorithm or empirical evaluation framework.
