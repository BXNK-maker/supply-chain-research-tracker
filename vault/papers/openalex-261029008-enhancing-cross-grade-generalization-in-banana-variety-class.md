# Enhancing Cross-Grade Generalization in Banana Variety Classification via Hybrid CNN-Transformers and Knowledge Self-Distillation

**Authors**: Khush Kataruka, Mohammad Muzammila, Zahid Akhtar, Raghavendra Ramachandra, Praveen Kumar Chandaliya
**Date**: 2026-10-21
**Paper ID**: [openalex:2610.29008](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29008)

## Summary

This paper addresses cross-grade banana variety classification under significant domain shift by proposing a dual-stream Swin ResNet hybrid framework enhanced with KL-divergence-based self-distillation. Evaluated on a large-scale dataset of 31,678 banana images using a Leave-One-Grade-Out protocol, the proposed approach achieves a mean accuracy of 81.25%. The results demonstrate that combining CNN and Transformer paradigms with self-distillation improves feature transferability, attention localization, and optimization stability for unseen quality grades.

## Key Contributions

- Proposes a dual-stream Swin ResNet hybrid framework combined with KL-divergence-based self-distillation for cross-grade banana variety classification under domain shift.
- Establishes a rigorous Leave-One-Grade-Out evaluation protocol on a large-scale dataset of 31,678 banana images spanning three varieties and three quality grades.
- Achieves a mean accuracy of 81.25%, outperforming individual CNN and Vision Transformer baseline models by effectively aligning probabilistic feature representations across streams.
