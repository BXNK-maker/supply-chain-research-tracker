Introduction
In vehicle routing problems (VRPs), the goal is to serve clients at minimum cost.
In the classical capacitated VRP (CVRP), demands are known before routes are constructed.
In applications such as fuel delivery, waste collection, and vending-machine replenishment, demands may be uncertain when a vehicle leaves the depot

The VRP with stochastic demands (VRPSD) models this uncertainty: the vehicle observes each client’s demand on its first visit

Exact algorithms.

Reoptimization.

Secomandi and Margot, (2009)

Goodson et al., (2013)

Goodson et al., (2016)
incorporated fixed-route restocking values into rollout policies to evaluate routing decisions.
Florio et al., (2022)
