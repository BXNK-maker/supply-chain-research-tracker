#### 1. Reimagining Vehicle Routing and Logistics via Neural & Topological Guidance
Vehicle Routing Problems (VRP) remain a prime testbed for modern AI, and several papers today push the boundaries of how neural networks interact with traditional solvers:
* **Addressing Cross-Scale Generalization:** Pointer networks trained via reinforcement learning traditionally struggle when deployed on scales or node densities different from their training distribution. By identifying root causes like *attention dispersion* and *graph context dilution*, researchers are introducing permutation-invariant graph-context aggregation and approach selectors that yield tangible routing cost reductions across unseen problem scales (2610.27936).
* **Topological Consistency:** Moving beyond autoregressive node selection with implicit routing, **TRCM (2610.27568)** explicitly models pairwise same-route probabilities and integrates topological entropy reweighting. This bridges the gap between neural token generation and the global structural requirements of routing graphs.
* **Breaking Column Generation Folklore:** In exact optimization, a long-standing intuition held that warm-starting column generation slows down branch-price-and-cut. Challenging this orthodoxy, **Learned Column Prefilling (2610.21001)** couples a neural edge scorer with Wentges smoothing prediction, achieving massive speedups (e.g., reducing root pricing time to 13.2% of vanilla solvers) and certifying complex scheduling and routing instances.

#### 2. Explainability and Robust Control under Uncertainty
As OR methods are deployed in high-stakes environments—such as medical distribution and last-mile operations—interpretability and robust dynamic modeling are paramount.
* **Coherent XAI:** Traditional Explainable AI (XAI) methods like LIME ignore the constraints of mathematical optimization. **CLEMO (2502.04840)** breaks new ground by delivering local explanations that simultaneously interpret objective values and decision variables *while preserving problem feasibility*.
* **Stochastic Control & Fairness:** In public health supply chains, balancing operating costs with fairness is notoriously tricky. By formulating a continuous-time approximation controlling the drift rate of a reflected Brownian motion—and solving it via deep splitting neural networks—researchers achieved significant cost reductions in Liberia's community health medication allocation while safeguarding fairness utility (2610.32051).
* **Action-Conditioned World Modeling:** **ActTime (2610.25764)** demonstrates that in time-series forecasting for traffic, energy, and inventory, factual accuracy and decision quality are fundamentally distinct objectives, emphasizing the need for action-conditioned world models that handle latent state updates and observation corrections robustly.
