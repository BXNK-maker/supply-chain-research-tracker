### 1. Operations Research, Routing, and Supply Chains
Routing and resource allocation remain central testbeds for combining classical operations research (OR) with machine learning. 
* **Generalization and Consistency in Neural Routing:** Two papers address the perennial Achilles' heel of neural combinatorial solvers: cross-scale generalization and local consistency. **From Comparison to Selection** tackles degradation in Pointer Networks when scaling from CVRP20 to CVRP100 by identifying node density shifts and graph context dilution, introducing a combination of entropy-based attention scaling and graph-context aggregation. Complementing this, **TRCM** injects topology-aware route-consistency modeling into autoregressive neural routing, utilizing topological entropy-based reweighting to manage class imbalance in customer co-assignment.
* **Exact Bounds and Applied Variants:** On the theoretical side, **Improved Approximation for Quota Trees** advances the state-of-the-art for the Unsplittable Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (UCVRP) with a deterministic $(5/2+\varepsilon)$-approximation algorithm. For practical distribution logistics, **Hybridizing 0/1 Knapsack and Priority-Aware TSP** presents a priority-biased framework that successfully merges economic utility (via squared client priority weights) with capacity-constrained routing.
* **Macro-level and Dynamic Control:** Moving beyond static vehicle routing, **A Dynamic Inventory Rationing Model** addresses last-mile medical delivery in Liberia by using deep neural networks and deep splitting methods to solve a reflected Brownian motion approximation, balancing fairness and operating costs. In macro-supply chains, **When Protection Hurts** introduces a decision-theoretic framework modeling "Supply Chain Cytokine Storms"—demonstrating mathematically how individual, defensive overreactions (hoarding) can trigger network-wide degradation via spectral stability conditions.

### 2. Time-Series World Modeling & Explainable AI
* **Action-Conditioned Forecasting:** **ActTime** steps into the realm of time-series world modeling by predicting how system states evolve under candidate interventions. Crucially, it learns exclusively from factual trajectories (without paired counterfactuals) and demonstrates that factual accuracy, intervention fidelity, and downstream decision quality are distinct capabilities often prone to objective conflict.
* **Coherent Explanations:** In mathematical optimization, explaining black-box decisions usually violates problem constraints. **CLEMO** bridges this gap by generating **Coherent Local Explanations for Mathematical Optimization**, ensuring that explanations for both objective values and decision variables respect the underlying structural feasibility of the problem.

### 3. Autonomous Discovery and Hardware-Software Co-Design
* **Persistent Autonomous Research:** A striking entry in autonomous systems is **Épi**, an agent designed to sustain scientific investigations across sessions using hierarchical memory, branching, and independent review. Its track record—producing 120 record-setting mathematical and algorithmic results (including progress on Tuza's conjecture for split graphs)—signals a maturation in autonomous research agents.
* **GPU-Resident Spatial Inspection & HLS:** In hardware and robotics, efficiency requires pushing parallelism to the metal. The **GPU-Resident Pipeline for Multi-Robot 3D Inspection** handles underwater structure scanning through a massive, end-to-end GPU-accelerated framework using custom CUDA kernels and 4D space-time path planning. Similarly, in high-level synthesis (HLS), **A State Augmented Approach to Parallel Modeling** demonstrates that making persistent architectural states and feedback control explicit in C++ lets designers overcome the concurrency bottlenecks typical of sequential behavioral models, outperforming standard algorithmic HLS baselines on an RV32I processor.

### Summary Takeaway
Today's research underscores a mature shift away from brute-force neural heuristics or rigid analytical solvers alone. The winning formula across these domains is **hybridization**: blending neural flexibility with structural priors—whether that means embedding topological entropy into routing, spectral stability into supply chains, reflected Brownian motions into inventory allocation, or explicit architectural state boundaries into hardware synthesis.
