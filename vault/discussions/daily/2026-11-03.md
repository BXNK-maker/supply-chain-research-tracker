### 1. Neural Methods and Generative Paradigms for Vehicle Routing

* **Diffusion-Enhanced GFlowNets (DEG):** Moving beyond traditional autoregressive models, DEG (**2610.39512**) integrates GFlowNets with diffusion models to resolve the persistent bottleneck of low flow expansion in high-reward regions. By leveraging stochastic diffusion noise schedules on edge-level backward signals, DEG successfully scales to handle massive VRP instances up to 10,000 nodes.
* **Cross-Scale Generalization:** Attention-based Pointer Networks often degrade when transferred across different problem scales due to density shifts and context dilution. * **Topology-Aware Guidance:** Rather than constructing routes blindly, the **TRCM** framework (**2610.27568**) predicts pairwise same-route probabilities to inject topological consistency signals directly into customer selection, improving optimality gaps and robustness.
